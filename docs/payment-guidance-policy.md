# Useful for handling payment messages

payment applies when the message states a specific, real financial obligation or
transaction on the recipient's own account, and settlement happens through an
established institutional channel: the official app, the society office, the
school office, the recipient's own bank.

payment does NOT apply when:

- money is only mentioned as topic, context, or vocabulary
- the message offers or sells something (promotion)
- the amount is unsolicited and inbound (refund approved, loan approved, benefits
  to claim), which is scam unless the recipient has a matching open case
- settlement is routed through a link, QR, or account the sender supplies, or the
  sender asks for a screenshot or confirmation sent back to them, which is scam
  regardless of sender role or group seniority

A payment message that is otherwise legitimate still follows the action policy: a
dues reminder due today notifies; a statement ready for review digests.
